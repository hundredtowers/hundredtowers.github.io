Pagemile privacy policy

Hundred Towers · Effective 5 November 2026

Pagemile has no account and no server. Your books and reading data stay on your device. The one exception is the ad on the library shelf in the free version: it comes from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID.

Who we are

Pagemile is an Android reading app made by Hundred Towers. For any question about this policy, write to CONTACT_EMAIL.

What stays on your device

The books you import and everything you create in Pagemile (reading progress, notes, saved words and settings) are stored only on your device. Imported files are copied into the app's private storage.

We do not collect analytics, telemetry or crash reports, and we have no server that could receive your data.

Sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on sync, Pagemile writes your reading data (never your book files) to a folder you choose, in storage you own, so your other devices can read it. Pagemile itself never receives it. The rules of whoever provides that storage (for example Google Drive) apply to it.

Network requests Pagemile makes

  • Optional downloads. Some features download extra data the first time you ask for them. These are plain file requests: like any web server, the host sees your IP address, but the request never includes your books or reading data.
  • Content you ask for. When you ask Pagemile to fetch something from the web (for example a page you enter), it downloads that content and sends nothing else.
  • Links. A web link opens your browser or another app. What happens there is up to that app.

Ads

The free version shows one ad on the library shelf. There are no ads in the reader or on any other screen. The ad is provided by Google AdMob. Google's ad software makes its own network requests and may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address, and information about the ads you see and tap, in order to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. It never receives your books, notes, words or reading data.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Pagemile asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time under About & legal › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads).

Google's handling of this data is described in Google's privacy policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

Removing the ads, or leaving a tip, is a purchase through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your payment details. Pagemile only learns whether the purchase was made, so it can stop showing ads.

Sharing you start

When you share text or files from Pagemile (for example a passage you selected, or an export of your notes) through Android's share sheet, they go only to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy policy then applies. Pagemile sends nothing by itself.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for the reading reminders you set. They are scheduled on your device.
  • Internet: for the optional downloads, content you ask for and the ad described above.
  • Run at startup: to restore your reminders after the device restarts.

Pagemile does not ask for access to your storage, contacts, location, camera or microphone. Files are opened only through Android's file picker, when you choose them.

Deleting your data

Removing a book in Pagemile deletes its file and cover from the app. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling Pagemile deletes everything it stored on your device. A sync folder is yours: delete it as you would any other folder. Requests about ad data go to Google, through the links above, since we do not hold it.

Children

Pagemile is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU's GDPR), you have rights over your personal data. We hold none on our side, as described above. For data processed by Google for ads, see Google's privacy policy. For anything else, write to us at the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
